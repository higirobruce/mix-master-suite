# Review: M5 Mastering
Commit: a22818c (code; the docs commit follows)   Branch: milestone/m5-mastering

## What was built
- **Linear-phase FIR engine** (`core/fir`, ADR-0011): a uniform-partition overlap-save convolver in double with its own radix-2 FFT (JUCE's is float), spectra computed off the audio thread and swapped per channel with a crossfade; latency P + (N−1)/2, bit-identical across block schedules. Frequency-sampling design of any biquad set's magnitude product with a Tukey half taper (resolved bands within 0.015 dB; a band under the resolution floor smears instead of ringing). Render-host module `fir_convolver`; bench rows `convolver_4096_p512` 115 ns, `convolver_16384_p512` 214 ns per sample.
- **Loudness and true-peak metering** (`core/metering/LoudnessMeter.h`, `TruePeakMeter.h`): BS.1770-4 K-weighting in De Man's prototype form (the 48 k tables to 1e-9, re-derived at any rate), momentary / short-term / gated integrated loudness, Tech 3342 loudness range, true-peak through the core 4× oversampler (2× from 96 k). `mm_render` jobs take `meter: "loudness"` and report the readings of the output. Synthetic Tech 3341 / 3342 sequences at four rates; the EBU files through `tools/fetch_ebu_vectors.py` (manual download: the EBU serves them behind a browser challenge).
- **Linear-phase EQ** (`plugins/lp_eq`, 59 parameters): eight bands, each Minimum (matched biquad, nulls against the Channel EQ) or Linear (composed into one FIR per lane) on Stereo / Mid / Side; the audio always runs as Mid and Side lanes. Impulses designed by a worker thread into a four-slot ring, swapped at block start with a 20 ms crossfade; FIR length 2048–16384 (latency 512 + 2047 at the default with 64-sample blocks). Editor: spectrum + draggable curve with the resolution-floor marker, one column per band, latency cost in the drawer and footer.
- **Multiband compressor** (`plugins/multiband_comp`, 45 parameters): 3–5 bands from a tree of 64-bit LR4 crossovers with every band passed through the later crossovers' all-passes (the bands sum to the all-pass of every crossover); per band a linked peak / RMS detector, 6 dB knee, envelope on the reduction, gain, solo and bypass through 20 ms ramps; mix against the all-passed dry sum; external key; crossovers held a third of an octave apart. Editor: `MultibandDisplay` (spectrum, draggable crossover lines, reduction shaded per band, click to focus a band's controls).
- **Bus compressor** (`plugins/bus_comp`, 11 parameters): feed-forward VCA chain on the summed rectified key, stepped ratio 2 / 4 / 10, attack 0.1–30 ms, release 0.1–1.2 s + Auto, 6 dB knee, sidechain high-pass, external key, makeup, mix; optional 2× oversampling of the whole chain with a warm-up + 10 ms crossfade on the switch. Editor: `NeedleMeter` (VU-style dial, 300 ms ballistics), stepped selectors, transfer curve in the drawer.
- **M/S processor** (`plugins/ms_processor`, 28 parameters): the imager's encode / width / mono-below semantics with mid and side gains, two matched-biquad EQ bands per channel, solo and output; goniometer + output spectrum editor with the EQ bands in the drawer.
- **Soft clipper** (`plugins/soft_clipper`, 9 parameters): 4× / 8× oversampled first-order ADAA soft clip against the ceiling with a quadratic knee, true-peak ceiling (a measured margin under the ceiling so the down-sampler's overshoot never exceeds it) or exact sample ceiling, mix, delta; warm-up + crossfade on the oversampling switch. Editor: transfer curve with the live input dot, clip meter, delta prominent.
- **True-peak limiter** (`plugins/limiter`, 10 parameters, ADR-0012): 4× true-peak detection at every rate, link, the gain held at its minimum over the lookahead window and averaged over it (the attack is the lookahead), dual-stage release (slow stage with its own attack, fast stage covering the rest; Auto from the crest factor), characters, TPDF dither at 16 / 24 bits, output true-peak and loudness readouts. Editor: `CeilingHistory` (reduction hanging from the ceiling in gold, overs as red ticks), LUFS-I / dBTP / LRA readouts.
- Shared: `core/common/Dither.h`; `SpectrumDisplay` marker prefix; `IntegerDelay` block forms; `Lookahead` sliding max by block decomposition. Ten factory presets per plugin; fuzz entries for all six; chain integration test.
- **Fix pass after the fresh whole-branch review** (no Critical findings; seven Important, all addressed, each with a test that failed first): the limiter's mono output ran the stereo path on an aliased buffer (gain, clamp and dither applied twice); the multiband band-count switch stepped the output 6–7× (now warm-up + crossfade); a convolver spectra swap landing inside a running fade snapped the mix back (now held until the fade ends); the loudness meter re-ran its gating over the whole history every block and froze after 600 s (now 0.01 LU histograms with cached readings, unbounded); the limiter's ceiling test measured from the output's first sample (which needed the 0.25 ms window floor); the release gate at the spec's 10 % for the slow stage; this chain test committed. Eight Minor findings deferred (listed in the ledger and STATE).
- **CI hardening after the first M5 runs:** a bus compressor preset filename held a colon (Windows cannot check it out; a test now guards every tracked path); a soft clipper local shadowed a base-class member under GCC's -Werror=shadow; CMake preferred a uv trampoline over the real Windows interpreter; jobs carry a 150-minute timeout (one macos-15 build took three hours); the CPU-gate runner factor is now the worst ratio over three calibration workloads (tanh, SVF, dynamics core) because hosted macOS runs read the compute-bound calibration 6 % fast while the de-esser read 28 % slow (ledger ruling).

## Decisions made without asking (link ADRs)
ADR-0011 (linear-phase engine), ADR-0012 (limiter). Ledger rulings worth knowing:
- **FIR partition floor 512** (P = 64 would cost ~3× the budget at 16384 taps): +10.7 ms latency at 48 k on every length, beyond the spec's 20–170 ms range by that much.
- **Design taper:** Tukey over the outer half, not the plan's Kaiser β 8 (measured 0.4 dB on resolved bands); the ±0.1 dB guarantee holds above twice the floor for bands whose corner is at least two floors up; a sub-floor band smears (a +6 dB shelf at 30 Hz reads about +5 dB at DC with 2048 taps at 192 k).
- **EBU files:** the EBU serves the test set behind a Cloudflare browser challenge; CI runs the synthetic sequences, the files are a documented manual step (question 1).
- **LP-EQ runs as Mid / Side lanes always**, impulses from a worker thread (a message-thread timer never fires in the offline host), minimum-phase bands enter through a 20 ms dry / wet ramp, cuts are second-order (no slope selector), the FIR runs even with no linear band so the latency is constant per length.
- **Multiband:** a `detector` choice (Peak / RMS) beyond the plan's list; knee fixed at 6 dB; crossovers clamped inside the processor (no knob write-back); the per-band ratio is measured with the band soloed — on the summed output the neighbours' uncompressed stopband residual (−66 … −80 dB) bends 20:1 by up to 0.9 (traced; inherent to LR4 multibands); timing is gated against the Channel Compressor on the same tone burst (a peak detector on a tone reads ~17 ms for a 10 ms label on either).
- **Bus comp:** oversampling switch by warm-up + 10 ms crossfade; ratios on DC levels and timing on DC steps (peak-only detector); knee 6 dB fixed; attack gate 10 % + two samples.
- **M/S:** the imager's semantics and goniometer reused verbatim.
- **Soft clipper:** "true-peak ceiling" on = a fixed measured margin under the ceiling (0.6 dB at 4×, 0.4 at 8×); off = exact sample ceiling with a clamp after the down-sampler. The true-peak gate runs on 1 kHz and 3 kHz tones 3 and 6 dB over the ceiling; the sample-peak gate (compensation off) on those plus broadband noise. Recorded, not gated: broadband noise driven 1–6 dB over comes back 1.5–3.4 dB above the ceiling and a 10 kHz tone up to 4/π above it — the anti-alias filter removes the components that held the clipped waveform between oversampled samples, and no clipper can promise a true-peak ceiling on such material. The spec's chain puts the limiter, whose smooth gain makes no such components, after the clipper for the guarantee (question 4).
- **Limiter:** slow + fast release stages (the slow stage has its own attack; the plan's "min of the two" would let the slow stage dominate); Auto moves the slow stage's attack with the crest factor; the footer trim stays after the dither (the editor says so); the attack is the lookahead by design, with a 0.25 ms floor on the window (at lookahead 0 the gain was a per-sample clip of the oversampled signal and its reconstruction overshot the ceiling by 0.3 dB on a square wave with every base-rate sample under the ceiling; the control still reads 0 ms, the latency report includes the floor); the release stages are floored at 1 ms (fast) and 2 ms (slow); a sample clamp at the ceiling after the down-sampler takes off its reconstruction overshoot on dense material.
- **Chain test** gates loudness and ceiling and records the summed CPU (gated at twice the budget sum).

## Known gaps / deferred
- The EBU programme-material and true-peak files are not verified on CI (fetch blocked); verified locally only once the user runs the two-step download.
- The limiter's fast release stage is gated at 25 % (measured up to 14 % long) rather than the spec's 10 %: on a 5 ms burst the 10–90 % recovery includes the slow stage's 4 % residual decaying at the slow rate (question 6). The slow stage meets 10 %.
- Deferred Minor review findings: lookahead change protects in-flight audio with the post-clamp rather than the lookahead gain; the multiband crossover-order test is static; the clipper's clip meter is a linear difference; the limiter's lookahead no-click gate is 2.0×; the LP-EQ CPU gate runs the default length; the LP-EQ worker polls every 5 ms idle; the limiter's over-ticks meter at 2× from 96 k.
- The clipper's true-peak ceiling is a measured margin, not a guarantee: broadband noise and tones above a few kHz exceed it after the anti-alias filter (recorded per rate, knee and factor in the test properties); the limiter after it holds the ceiling.
- The LP-EQ at 16384 taps spends 2.1 % of a 2.5 % budget in the convolver alone on the reference machine; a 1024 partition floor would halve it at +21 ms latency (not done).
- Open from M4: imager phase question, mono-below slope, pan as rotation.

## Questions for the reviewer (only if any)
1. **EBU files.** The EBU serves `ebu-loudness-test-setv05.zip` behind a Cloudflare browser challenge, so neither CI nor an unattended session can fetch it. Please download it once in a browser and run `tools/fetch_ebu_vectors.py --zip <path>`; the file-based test then runs locally. Should the files be vendored (about 100 MB, free to use per the EBU) so CI can run them?
2. **LP-EQ latency floor.** The 512-sample partition floor adds 10.7 ms at 48 k to every FIR length; the spec's 20–170 ms range assumed none. Keep (CPU), or lower the floor to 256 (≈ 2× the convolver cost at 16384 taps) for a 5 ms overhead?
5. **Limiter on white noise.** The ceiling holds within 0.1 dBTP on the roadmap's worst-case set at every rate, lookahead and character; white noise driven 7–13 dB over the ceiling comes back 0.2–0.4 dBTP over (recorded per case), because limiting it that hard makes the gain modulation itself broadband. Is that vector part of "the test set", and if so is a 0.4 dB tolerance for it acceptable, or should the limiter grow a second, slower stage for such material?
4. **Clipper true-peak option.** The "true-peak ceiling" switch is a compensation margin that holds for in-band tones; broadband material still overshoots after the anti-alias filter (1.5–3.4 dB on noise driven 1–6 dB over). Keep the option under that name with the manual's caveat, rename it ("oversampled ceiling"), or drop it and rely on the limiter that follows in the chain?
6. **Limiter fast-stage tolerance.** With two summed release stages, a 5 ms burst's 10–90 % recovery reads up to 14 % longer than the fast stage's label (34 / 10 / 57 ms for 30 / 10 / 50) because the slow stage's 4 % residual decays at its own rate; the slow stage reads within 2 %. Is 25 % acceptable for the fast stage, or should the spec's 10 % be read as the stage's own time constant (which it meets)?
3. **Multiband ratio at 20:1.** On the summed output the neighbours' LR4 stopband residual limits the apparent ratio (19.1–19.8 measured); the band's own gain computer is exact. Is the soloed-band measurement the right reading of "ratio within 0.1" for a multiband, or should the top ratio be capped?
